Return Policy

Eligibility Window
- Most items are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be returned in original condition unless damaged in transit.

Damaged Items
- If an item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 7 days of delivery.
- Damaged item claims are eligible for a full refund or free replacement after verification.
- Verification may include photos of product damage and packaging.
- Return shipping for damaged items is covered by the company.

Non-Returnable Items
- Final-sale items and customized products are not eligible for return.
- Hygiene-sensitive goods are non-returnable once opened.

Refund Timelines
- Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days.
- Original shipping fees are refundable only when the return reason is damaged or incorrect item.

Exchange Policy
- Exchanges are allowed for size or color variants when stock is available.
- If replacement stock is unavailable, a refund is issued.
